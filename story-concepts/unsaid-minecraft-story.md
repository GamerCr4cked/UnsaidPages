## 1. Concept & Structure

- **Story kya hai:** Meri (tamim0047) real Minecraft + Discord life story — kaise log mile, dost bane, kuch reh gaye, kuch kho gaye.
- **POV:** Main character **mai hun**. Kisi aur ka POV nahi — jab dusron ka aata hai toh **sab ka ek saath** aata hai (ensemble scenes), warna mera.
- **No side characters** — har banda jo entry kare, proper intro + details milenge.
- **Format:** Multi-season nahi. **Ek single unified story, cinema-vibe, arc-based.**
  - Har arc = ek era/server/phase
  - No hard season breaks
  - Movie/series vibe — title card openings, dramatic closings, montages, parallel cuts, cliffhangers
- **Thor SMP** = story ka pehla major arc. Thor ke andar internal seasons hain (Thor S1, S2, S3). Thor ke aage bhi aur Thor seasons aayenge baad me (jab timeline uss tak pahunchegi), aur beech me dusre servers/eras bhi aayenge.
- **Language:** Fully multi-language (Hinglish default + Bangla + English + jo jahan natural ho), kyunki ye real life story hai. Alag language ka **Hindi meaning brackets me ya kisi aur tareeke se** likha jayega.
- **Tone:** Fully unfiltered, but engaging — kuch cheezein filter karenge taaki bore na ho.
- **Emotional core:** "Unsaid goodbyes." Bohot log aaye, dost bane, phir suddenly gayab ho gaye (Coolmentis, KING_OF_STAR, etc.) — ye repo ke naam (**UnsaidPages**) ke saath match karta hai.
- **Timeline so far:** 2021 → late 2023 (Thor SMP arc covers this). Aage ka time ban raha hai.

## 6. Narrative Rules

Jab actually writing shuru karenge, ye rules follow karenge:

1. **POV** — Mera (tamim/ITsUnknown_Boy). Ensemble scenes me sab ka ek saath show karenge, warna bas mera.
2. **No side characters** — har entry wale ko proper intro do. Sirf naam drop nahi.
3. **Multi-language natural** — Hinglish + Bangla + English + jo jahan natural. Alag language ka meaning bracket me ya inline translation.
4. **No spoilers** — future ke naam changes, future ke events, **pehle se hint nahi**. Reveal organically jab moment aaye.
5. **Cinema-vibe arc-based** — title cards, montages, parallel cuts, cliffhangers. No strict chapter length.
6. **Filter lightly** — mostly unfiltered, but engagement ke liye kuch trim/adjust karenge.
7. **Emotional consistency** — "unsaid goodbyes" theme ko beech beech me subtly surface karte chalenge (Dakshop fade, Coolmentis loss, KING_OF_STAR disappearance, Speed distance).
8. **Real naam use** — gamer tags real honge, but name-change evolution story ke moments me reveal.
9. **Slow build** — rush nahi. User ne kaha kahani abhi **20% hi hui hai**, matlab bohot detail aayega.
10. **One chapter at a time** — writing karte waqt Edit tool se ek ek chapter likhenge, saath pe pura nahi (hang avoid karne ke liye).
