Chapter 1 — The Deepest Frost

Alaska ki thand sirf ek temperature nahi hai; yeh ek bhautik maujoodgi hai. Yeh underground excavation sites ke reinforced steel darwazon par dabav banati hai, ventilation shafts ke andar rengti hui aati hai, aur un logon ki haddiyon ke majja tak utar jaati hai jo itni kathor jagah par raat ki shift mein kaam karne ki himmat karte hain.

Kenai Peninsula ke jami hui tundra se do mile neeche, hawa mein basi hui lohe ki dhool, geeli mitti aur teen levels upar goonj rahe bhaari generators se nikalne wale diesel exhaust ki halki, teekhi gandh ghuli hui thi. Woh bahut kam bolta tha. Woh bas duniya ke kinaare par apna kaam karne wala ek aadmi tha. Ya kam se kam sabhi aisa hi maante the.
