Chapter 1: Appeal

Raat ke 2:14 ho rahe the. Kamre mein sannata itna gehra tha ki mujhe apne mechanical keyboard ke har ek key-press ki awaaz shishe ki tarah toot-ti hui mehsoos ho rahi thi. Monitor ki blue-ish light mere chehre pe padh rahi thi, jisse main ek ghost ki tarah lag raha tha. Discord ka interface khula tha—wohi familiar, dark theme—ek aisi jagah jo kabhi mera home thi, ab ek aisi jagah jahan main bas ek ghost banke reh gaya tha. "Submit Appeal." Do chote shabd, par inke peeche paanch mahine ka intezaar, paanch mahine ki frustration, aur ek poori dosti ka future daav pe tha.

Meri fingers keyboard pe thi, par main click nahi kar paa raha tha. Har ek click ka weight itna zyada tha ki mere haath kaanp rahe the. Maine niche dekha—Alone—mere saath VC mein tha. Silence itni gehri thi ki mujhe uske saans lene ki awaaz tak apne headphones mein sunayi de rahi thi. Hum dono paanch mahine se isi moment ka wait kar rahe the. Hum dono jaante the ki ye appeal koi ordinary request nahi thi; ye ek maut se ladne ka raasta tha—ek aise rishte ki maut, jo abhi zinda toh tha, par saansein nahi le raha tha.

Maine Discord ke interface ko ghoora. Har ek pixel jaise mujhpe has raha ho. "Appeal submitted" ka button dabana kitna aasaan tha, par us appeal ko submit karne ke baad jo sannata aata, wahi asli darr tha. Kya ye appeal use wapas layegi? Ya kya ye appeal humein permanently us stranger se door kar degi? Maine socha ki kya KingOP ko pata bhi hai ki hum yahan baith ke uska purana ID wapas lane ki koshish kar rahe hain. Shayad use farak bhi nahi padta. Shayad wo Zynx ban ke bohot khush hai. Shayad use Zoe ki duniya mein humse zyada importance mil rahi hai.

Waqt jaise ruk gaya tha. Har ek second ek ghante ke barabar lag raha tha. Maine apne headphones utare aur paani ka glass uthaya. Gala sukha hua tha. Paanch mahine pehle jab wo account suspend hua tha, tab humne socha tha ki kuch dinon mein unban ho jayega. Par din hafton mein badle, hafte mahino mein, aur wo temporary suspension permanent nightmare ban gaya. KingOP ka wo account sirf ek login nahi tha—wo hamari teen saal ki purani dosti ka locker tha jismein humari saari hansi, saari ladaiyan, aur saare secrets band the.

Alone
Bhai... sach mein lagta hai ye work karega? Humne pehle bhi toh kiya tha. Woh log reply tak nahi karte. Ab paanch mahine ho gaye, Unknown. Ab kya change hoga? Hum kyun khud ko itna dard de rahe hain?
Uski awaaz mein wo bachpana nahi tha jo OPSMP ke dinon mein hua karta tha. Ab wo serious tha. Bohot zyada serious. Uske voice modulation mein wo thakawat thi, jo sirf tab aati hai jab tum baar baar haar chuke ho. Wo ab thak chuka tha—hamare mission se, is wait se, aur is dosti ke incomplete hone se.

Pata nahi, Alone. Par ye last option hai. Agar is baar unka reply nahi aaya, toh... toh samajh lena KingOP permanently Zynx ban gaya hai. Phir koi wapas nahi aayega. Phir wo Moonlit server aur Zoe ki duniya hi uska ghar ban jayegi. Humein pata hai ki wo wahan khush nahi hai, par wo khud ko itna busy kar chuka hai ki use humari zarurat tak mehsoos nahi hoti. Hum bas try kar sakte hain, bhai. Haar maan lene se toh behtar hai ki hum ek baar aur gir ke dekhein.

Hum dono jaante the ki KingOP ka purana ID sirf ek login nahi tha. Wo uske us persona ki keys thi—wo persona jo mazaak karta tha, jo hosting sambhalta tha, jo humein roast karta tha. Jab wo ID suspend hua, usne uske saath hi humari dosti ka wo purana version bhi suspend kar diya tha. Zynx? Zynx toh bas ek shell tha—ek aisa banda jo physically humare saath tha, par mentally Moonlit server aur Zoe ki duniya mein kho gaya tha. Usne humein wahan se nikaal diya tha, par wo khud wahan phasa hua tha.

Mere dimaag mein wo saari moments ghoom rahi thi—kaise ek suspension ne mere best friend ko ek stranger mein badal diya. Maine cursor submit button pe leke gaya. Har millisecond ek ghante jaisa lag raha tha. Kya main sahi kar raha hoon? Kya ye appeal usse wapas layegi, ya ye humein hamesha ke liye door kar degi? Maine apni aankhein band ki. Maine socha ki agar ye click fail hua, toh kya bachega? Kya hamare paas koi aur option hoga? Shayad nahi. Shayad yehi humara aakhri mukaam tha.

Kash ye click sirf ek appeal na ho. Kash ye click humein wapas wahi OPSMP ke dinon mein le jaaye. Kash wo purani bakchodi wapas aa jaaye. Kash KingOP wapas aa jaaye. Kash wo 'Zoe's Bodyguard' wala mask utar jaye aur wo hamara wahi purana best friend wapas aa jaye jiske saath humne hosting ka sapna dekha tha.

Alone ki saans ruki hui thi. Usne mic mute kiya, shayad wo bhi prarthana kar raha tha. Maine click kar diya.

Screen pe 'Processing...' dikha, aur phir 'Appeal Submitted.' Ab hum bas wait kar sakte the. Us sannate mein, mujhe wo sab yaad aa raha tha jo yahan tak leke aaya. Wo din jab hum sirf teen strangers the, aur tab se aaj tak ka ye safar. Hamari dosti ka graph—jo ThorSMP ke raid se shuru hua, Alone Hosting ke peak pe gaya, aur ab ek suspension ke andhere mein kho gaya tha.

Kyunki kuch kahaniyan tab khatam nahi hoti jab tum door ho jaate ho. Kahaniyan tab khatam hoti hain jab tum unhe wapas paane ki koshish karna chhod dete ho. Humne abhi haar nahi maani thi.

Chapter 2: ThorSMP

Tab sab kuch bohot simple tha. Zindagi mein hosting, database, zoe, ya identity crisis jaise cheezein nahi thi. Bas Minecraft tha, ek village, aur totems ki zarurat thi. ThorSMP ka wo din mujhe aaj bhi yaad hai—it was chaotic, messy, aur exactly waisa tha jaisa hona chahiye. Main raid ke liye spam kar raha tha. I was desperate. Mere paas resources nahi the aur village raid ke liye humein ek team chahiye thi. Tabhi screen pe chat box mein ek naam pop hua: KingOP.

KingOP
Bro, main help karta hu. coordinates bhej.

Wo akela aaya. Maine socha tha ki raid ke liye 3-4 logon ki team lagegi, par KingOP ne bina kuch kahe mere saath raid puri ki. He was efficient, calm, aur sabse zaroori—he didn't steal the totems. ThorSMP mein jab aksar log loot ke liye ek dusre ko dhokha dete the, KingOP ka wo approach mere liye shock tha. Raid khatam hone ke baad hum ek mountain top pe khade the, sunset dekh rahe the, aur tabhi humne baatein shuru ki. Wo sunset humari dosti ki shuruat thi—ek aisa moment jahan humein laga ki ye koi temporary teammate nahi hai, balki ek aisa partner hai jiske saath main safe feel kar sakta hoon.

Us raid ke baad, humne apni Discord IDs share ki. Us raat ki baat-cheet mein wo trust build hua jo maine online kisi ke saath feel nahi kiya tha. Woh sirf ek teammate nahi tha; woh samajh gaya tha ki mujhe kaise playstyle pasand hai. Humne ghanto baatein ki, par minecraft ke baare mein kam aur zindagi ke baare mein zyada. Pata nahi kab raat ke 3 baj gaye, par humein pata tha ki humein ek dusre ka partner mil gaya hai. Tab wo 'KingOP' tha, ek aisa ladka jo sirf gamer nahi, balki ek insaan tha jo support system ban sakta tha. Humari dosti tab ek saaf chashme ki tarah thi—koi confusion nahi, koi hidden agenda nahi. Bas hum the, aur humari baatein.

ThorSMP ke wo din bade niraale the. Har taraf bases banna, raiding karna, aur ek dusre ko troll karna. KingOP hamesha aage rehta tha jab bhi mujhe items ki zarurat hoti thi. Usne kabhi credit nahi manga. Wo bas silent support system tha. Hum dono ghanto ghanto tak voice channel me baith ke bas game khelte rehte the bina kisi boredom ke. Tabhi humein realize hua ki online dosti sirf pixels tak limited nahi hoti—wahan bhi asli emotions aur asli dharhadte dil hote hain.

Chapter 3: OPSMP

Kuch hafte baad, KingOP ne mujhe OPSMP join karne ka invite diya. Main join hua—wahan ka infrastructure bohot zyada sophisticated tha. KingOP ne wahan kaafi setup kar liya tha. Jaise hi me enter hua, bina kisi "hi-hello" ke, mere screen pe ek notification aaya: "You have been promoted to Owner."

Maine chat check kiya, wahan ek aur player tha—Alone. Uski role bhi 'Owner' thi. Wo server ka environment bohot different tha. Alone wahan ke system ko manage kar raha tha, aur uska approach bohot chaotic tha. Woh commands ke saath khelta tha, admins ke saath mazaak karta tha, aur pura server atmosphere ko light rakhta tha.

Alone
Bhai, ye naya Owner kaun hai? King, tu kisko ghusa raha hai apne server mein? Ye toh naya lag raha hai.
KingOP
Chill mar, ye Unknown hai. Ab se hum teeno hi sambhalenge sab. Trust me, he knows his stuff.

Alone ka wo roasting approach mujhe pasand aaya. He was suspicious but funny. Maine pehli baar Alone se baat ki thi, aur wahi se humari trio bani. Tab humein nahi pata tha ki ye teeno ka bond aage jaake "Alone Hosting" jaisa kuch bada khada karne wala hai. Hum bas teen players the jo ek saath milke duniya conquer karna chahte the—ya shayad bas ek saath rehna chahte the. Tab humhari dosti sirf ek game server tak limited nahi thi; hum teeno ne milke ek aisi community banayi thi jahan humara control tha, humari rules thi, aur humari bakchodi thi.

OPSMP mein humne apne base banaye, automated farms setup kiye, aur pure server par apna dominance establish kiya. Alone hamesha chat mein aakar random logon ke saath bezti wali baatein karta tha, aur KingOP background mein plugins configure karta rehta tha. Main economy aur permissions sambhalta tha. Hum teeno ki tuning aisi baith gayi thi ki bina kuch bole ek dusre ka intent samajh jaate the. Raat ko VC join karna aur subah tak haste rehne—wo humari daily routine ban chuki thi.

Chapter 4: Alone Hosting

Alone Hosting ki shuruat koi bade announcement se nahi hui thi. Wo ek late-night decision tha—VC mein baith ke jab hum teeno bored ho rahe the, aur humein laga ki market mein jitni hosting companies hain, wo sab users ko sirf loot rahi hain. Sab scam kar rahe the, aur humein laga hum kuch "real" aur "honest" kar sakte hain. KingOP tab ek alag hi zone mein tha—usne decide kiya tha ki humein ab sirf gamers nahi, balki service providers banna hai.

KingOP
Brooo, ek idea hai. Minecraft hosting ka scene bohot messed up hai, sab scam karte hain. Maine socha, kyu na hum khud ka kuch karein? Alone Hosting. Free, reliable, aur apun log khud handle karenge. Log scam ho rahe hain, aur humein unhe ek genuine home dena hai.
Discord DM pe invite link ke saath aaya, confident jaise ye koi revolutionary project ho. Uski awaaz mein wo energy thi jo humein bhi inspire kar gayi.

Maine bina soche link click kiya aur join ho gaya. Alone Hosting ka server ka admin panel khula tha mere screen pe. Ye sirf ek project nahi tha; ye humari pehli attempt thi ek real startup ki. KingOP ne apna pura dimag lagaya tha—domain, VPS, panel, funding—sab usne arrange kiya. Hum bas executing partners the. Lekin security ka scene... wahan humari asli dosti ki testing shuru hui. KingOP security ko leke bohot obsessive tha, shayad isliye kyunki wo nahi chahta tha ki humari mehnat scam ho jaye.

Alone ka kaam tha server ko promote karna aur community ko entertain rakhna. Wo har naye user ke saath aisi baatein karta tha ki log hste-hste paagal ho jayein. Aur mera kaam tha backend support, tickets handle karna, aur bugs fix karna. Hum teeno ek machine ke 3 gears the. Agar ek bhi gear rukta, toh poora system jam ho jata tha. Din-raat tickets aate the. Kabhi kabhi raat ke 4 baje server crash ho jata, aur hum teeno turant VC join karke fix karne lagte the. Wo pressure bohot zyada tha, par us pressure mein ek mazaa tha—ek aisi bonding thi jo sirf dosto mein ho sakti hai.

Chapter 5: VC Chaos

Alone Hosting ke dinon mein hamari dosti apne sabse golden peak par thi. Din bhar hosting manage karne ke baad, raat ko hum teeno ek private voice channel mein ghus jaate the. Wahan koi rules nahi the, koi client tension nahi thi—bas hum teeno aur hamari endless bakchodi thi.

Alone hamesha mic ke paas aakar aisi ajeeb awaazein nikalta tha ki humari hasi chhoot jaati thi. KingOP apne coding errors pe gussa karne ki acting karta, aur main beech mein unhe roast karta tha. Hum ghanto tak YouTube videos ek sath sync karke dekhte the, ya phir random public servers par jaakar trollers ki band bajaate the.

Alone
Bhai sun, ek naya ladka aaya hai general chat mein. Kehta hai ki wo world ka best Java developer hai. Maine usko Pterodactyl panel ka basic error pucha, bhai wo offline ho gaya ek second mein! Hahaha!
KingOP
Abe chhod na usko, idhar dekh maine naye node pe SSD raid configuration set kiya hai. Ab speed double ho jayegi. Unknown, tera backup script properly run ho raha hai na?
Unknown
Haan bhai, sab automation me set hai. Tu tension mat le. Par pehle ye bata, Alone itni der se jo gana gaa raha hai, iska mic mute kyu nahi kar raha koi? Kaunsa torture hai ye?

Wo raatein aisi thi ki lagta tha waqt wahin tham jaye. Hum teeno alag-alag shehron mein the, alag-alag background se the, par screen ke uss paar hum ek hi parivaar the. Ek dusre ke dukh-sukh samajhna, college ya school ki frustration nikalna, aur future ke sapne dekhna—ye sab hamari un raaton ka hissa tha. Humein lagta tha ki ye dosti kabhi kamzor nahi padegi, kyunki humne ise mehnat se banaya tha.

Chapter 6: Server War

Har successful project ki tarah, Alone Hosting ko bhi jealous logon aur rival communities ka samna karna pada. Ek hafte aisi aayi jab ek competing hosting group ne humare servers par DDoS attacks aur bot spam shuru kar diya. Panel lag ho raha tha, CPU usage 100% pahunch chuka tha, aur users ke tickets ka dher lag gaya tha.

Tab humne dekhi KingOP ki asli capability. Wo panic nahi hua. Usne turant firewall rules tighten kiye, bad IPs ko block kiya, aur cloudflare mitigation on kiya. Alone ne community ko calm down karne ke liye announcement channel par memes daalne shuru kar diye taaki panic na faile.

KingOP
Bhai log, attack aane do. Main baitha hu na. Ek bhi node down nahi hoga. Unknown, tu database logs check kar, koi data breach toh nahi hua hai. Alone, tu public chat handle kar. Let's show them who we are.
Unknown
Database secure hai, King. Maine encryption enable kar di hai. Ye saale jitna attack karenge, utna hi strong hamara system banega.

Wo 48 ghante humne bina soye, energy drinks pee kar code likhte hue nikale. Attackers haar maan gaye. Alone Hosting aur zyada powerful hokar ubarhi. Us war ke baad hum teeno ka confidence alag level par tha. Humein laga ki hum invincible hain—ki hamari dosti ko koi hila nahi sakta. Par hum nahi jaante the ki external dushman se zyada dangerous wo andar ke chote badlaav hote hain jo bina aawaz ke aate hain.

Chapter 7: The Ticket

Wo ek aam Tuesday tha. Alone Hosting ke support channel pe ek naya ticket generate hua. Ticket ka subject tha: "Server lag issue / need help." Main free tha, isliye maine ticket claim kar liya. Discord pe jab maine user profile dekhi, toh naam tha Bunny.

Bunny
Hello! Mera server baar baar crash ho raha hai. Pata nahi kya error aa raha hai console mein. Kya aap check kar sakte ho?
Unknown
Hi Bunny. Don't worry, main check karta hu. Console log ka pastebin link bhejo ya screenshot dalo.

Maine error fix kar diya—wo bas ek plugin conflict tha. Bunny ne bohot thanks bola. Wo chat wahi khatam ho sakti thi, par Bunny ne server pe active rehna shuru kar diya. She was friendly, active, aur thodi curious. Dheere dheere wo Alone Hosting ke general chat ki regular ban gayi. Alone uske saath mazaak karne laga, aur KingOP bhi uske tickets ya queries pe instantly reply karne laga. Tab tak sab normal tha—ek naya user community mein add hua tha.

Lekin dheere-dheere dynamics shift hone lagi. Bunny ko staff role mil gaya kyunki wo active rehti thi aur logon ki help karti thi. Staffing team mein aane ke baad, uska interaction KingOP ke saath sabse zyada badh gaya. Wo dono late night tak VC mein baith ke baatein karne lage. Pehle KingOP sirf humare (Alone aur mere) saath VC mein rehta tha, par ab wo Bunny ke saath alag se time spend karne laga tha. Alone aur maine shuru mein isko ignore kiya—humein laga normal friend ban rahi hai community ki.

Alone
Bhai, ek baat notice ki? King aajkal humare saath kam aur us Bunny ke saath zyada rehta hai. Kuch toh chal raha hai scene.
Unknown
Rehne de na bhai, kya farak padta hai? Community member hai, help karti hai server ki. Tu kyu overthink kar raha hai?

Par overthinking nahi thi, wo ek subtle shift ki shuruat thi. Bunny ke aane se server pe ek alag energy aa gayi thi—kuch log uske favour mein the, kuch against. Aur KingOP... KingOP dheere-dheere humse cut off hone laga. Jab bhi hum kuch discuss karne ke liye bulate, wo kehta "Busy hoon, baad mein baat karte hain." Wo "baad mein" kabhi nahi aata tha. Aur yehi wo pehla crack tha jo hamari strong trio ke beech mein aaya tha. Ek chota sa support ticket, jo ek error fix karne ke liye khula tha, wo dheere-dheere hamari dosti ke architecture ko andar se khokhla kar raha tha.

Chapter 8: Drift

Dosti mein jab darar aati hai, toh wo ek din mein nahi aati. Wo dheere-dheere aati hai—chote chote ignore kiye gaye messages se, late night VC se gayab rehne se, aur un ankahe sawaalon se jo gale mein fass ke reh jaate hain. KingOP ab humse baat karne se bachne laga tha. Jab bhi hum VC join karte, wo ya toh mute rehta ya phir jaldi se disconnect kar deta. Alone aur main aapas mein baith ke sochte ki aakhir hua kya hai. Kya humne kuch galat bola? Kya hosting management mein humse koi galti ho gayi? Par koi jawab nahi milta tha. KingOP apni ek alag duniya mein busy ho chuka tha—ek aisi duniya jismein Bunny thi, aur baaki log the, par Alone aur Unknown ke liye koi jagah nahi thi.

Alone

Unknown
Pata nahi, Alone. Mujhe bhi samajh nahi aa raha. Woh ab pehle jaisa nahi raha. Lagta hai use humari zarurat hi nahi hai ab.

Aur tabhi wo bomb phuta jisne sab kuch khatam kar diya. Discord ne KingOP ka main account suspend kar diya! Reason? Kuch policy violation ya mass report ka chakkar tha (shayad kuch server drama ya Bunny ke aas-paas ke disputes ki wajah se). Uska pura online existence, uske saare friends, uska status—sab ek jhatke mein zero ho gaya. Aur jab use sabse zyada humari zarurat honi chahiye thi, tab usne humse aur doori bana li. Usne socha ki hum uski halat pe has rahe hain, jabki sachai ye thi ki hum hi the jo use wapas lane ke liye raat-raat bhar jag rahe the.

Chapter 9: Zynx

Chapter 10: Last Login

Alone
Bhai... kya aaya? Jaldi bata!

Maine screen pe dekha. Wo status padh ke mere gale mein ek ajeeb si ruddh aawaz fass gayi. Appeal accept ho chuki thi! KingOP ka purana, suspended account unban kar diya gaya tha! Wo ID, jo paanch mahine se dead padi thi, ab zinda thi.
