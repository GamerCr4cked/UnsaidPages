Chapter 3: Alone Hosting

Alone Hosting ki shuruat koi bade announcement se nahi hui thi. Wo ek late-night decision tha—VC mein baith ke jab hum teeno bored ho rahe the, aur humein laga ki market mein jitni hosting companies hain, wo sab users ko sirf loot rahi hain. Sab scam kar rahe the, aur humein laga hum kuch "real" aur "honest" kar sakte hain. KingOP tab ek alag hi zone mein tha—usne decide kiya tha ki humein ab sirf gamers nahi, balki service providers banna hai.

KingOP
Brooo, ek idea hai. Minecraft hosting ka scene bohot messed up hai, sab scam karte hain. Maine socha, kyu na hum khud ka kuch karein? Alone Hosting. Free, reliable, aur apun log khud handle karenge. Log scam ho rahe hain, aur humein unhe ek genuine home dena hai.
Discord DM pe invite link ke saath aaya, confident jaise ye koi revolutionary project ho. Uski awaaz mein wo energy thi jo humein bhi inspire kar gayi.

Maine bina soche link click kiya aur join ho gaya. Alone Hosting ka server ka admin panel khula tha mere screen pe. Ye sirf ek project nahi tha; ye humari pehli attempt thi ek real startup ki. KingOP ne apna pura dimag lagaya tha—domain, VPS, panel, funding—sab usne arrange kiya. Hum bas executing partners the. Lekin security ka scene... wahan humari asli dosti ki testing shuru hui. KingOP security ko leke bohot obsessive tha, shayad isliye kyunki wo nahi chahta tha ki humari mehnat scam ho jaye.

KingOP
Dekho, data encryption is crucial. Database ka password bohot strong hona chahiye. No 'admin123' or any basic shit. Bhai, security sabse upar hai. Is project pe humara reputation daav pe hai.
Serious tone, jaise kisi tech conference mein lecture de raha ho.
Alone
Bhai, tu tension mat le. Main password set kar deta hu — 'password69'. Mast secure hai, koi guess nahi kar paayega.
Kaminey wali hasi, uski awaaz mein wahi purana mazaak aur confidence.

KingOP ek second ke liye chup hua, aur phir hum dono—main aur KingOP—zor se hasne lage. Discord ki puri VC laughter se bhar gayi. Ye wahi moment tha jahan humein laga ki hum kuch bada kar sakte hain, aur humari chemistry perfect thi. Us 'password69' wale joke ne humari saari tension ko ek minute mein khatam kar diya tha. Tab humein nahi pata tha ki ye joke humari dosti ka ek aisa pillar ban jayega, jise hum hamesha yaad rakhenge, chahe dosti kitni bhi complex ho jaye.

Hosting ke struggles real the. "Bhai server start nahi ho raha", "Bhai panel ka access nahi mil raha." Hum teeno ne milke raat-raat bhar jaag ke in errors ko solve kiya. Tabhi pata chala ki KingOP planning mein master hai, main execution mein, aur Alone... Alone cheezon ko zinda rakhne mein. Woh humein hasata tha jab hum frustrated hote the. Wo wahi banda tha jo server downtime ke waqt bhi meme bhej ke mood light kar deta tha. Raat ko 3-4 baje jab servers crash hote, hum teeno VC mein baith ke ek dusre ko roast karte hue fix karte the—wo frustration bhi ek bond ban gaya tha.

Humne ghanto spend kiye documentation padhne mein, panels configure karne mein. Kabhi-kabhi, jab sab kuch fail ho raha hota, Alone ka wo 'password69' wala joke wapas yaad aa jata. Hum hosting ke kings the, aur hume koi nahi rok sakta tha. Humari dosti tab sirf ek screen ke peeche thi, par wo screens humein ek dusre ke itna close le aayi thi ki hume real-life dosti ki zarurat hi mehsoos nahi hoti thi. Humare liye, wo digital world hi sabse zyada real tha.

KingOP tab bohot dedicated tha. Wo coding, server stability, aur funding—har cheez ka burden apne kandhon pe utha raha tha. Hum sirf backend support the, par wo... wo visionary tha. Humein kabhi lagta nahi tha ki ye hosting service ek din humari dosti ko kharab kar degi. Humein bas lagta tha ki hum teeno milke duniya ka har issue solve kar sakte hain. Raat ki wo silence mein, bas humare keyboard ki clicking aur humari haasi thi. Woh waqt bohot simple tha—wo waqt jab dosti ka matlab sirf saath baithna aur hosting manage karna tha.

Ek baar server database corrupt ho gaya. KingOP panic kar raha tha, "Bhai, pura user data uda diya toh hum scammer ban jayenge!" Main aur Alone usse bol rahe the, "Chill kar bhai, backup hai!" Wo panic mein 'password69' ko 'secure_password_123' mein badalne ki koshish kar raha tha, aur Alone uske system settings ke saath mazaak kar raha tha. Humari dosti ka wahi peak tha—jab humein laga ki hum teeno milke duniya ka har issue solve kar sakte hain, chahe wo technical ho ya emotional. Hum nahi jaante the ki ye dosti ka peak tha, aur yahan se bas drift shuru hone wali thi.
