Chapter 6: Technologiaa, Zynx, and Moonlit

Alone Hosting ke collapse ke baad, Discord ek sunsaan sheher jaisa ho gaya tha. Notification bell ab nahi bajti thi, aur VC mein bas main aur Alone bache the. Hum dono ke beech wo silence thi—ek aisi silence jo tab aati hai jab tum apne sabse ache dost ko khone ke baad bhi usse baat karna chahte ho. Humne ek nayi team banayi—Technologiaa. Ye hosting nahi thi; ye chaos tha. Hum Minecraft servers ko dominate karte, hacker-minded banter karte, aur pura server atmosphere ko ruin kar dete—just for fun. Ye humara coping mechanism tha, ek aisi power trip jisse humein mehsoos hota tha ki hum abhi bhi control mein hain, chahe dosti haath se fisal rahi ho.

Alone
Bhai, wo server dekha? Uski economy humne 10 minute mein destroy kar di. Ekdum khatam! Hahaha! Technologiaa zindabad!
Alone ki awaaz mein wo bachpana nahi tha jo Flexxy ke time pe hua karta tha. Wo ab aggressive tha, loud tha. Usne apna naam wapas 'Alone' kar liya tha, jaise wo apni purani identity ko hold karna chahta ho.
Me
Haa bhai. Unhe pata bhi nahi chala ki exploit kahan se hua. Wo admins rote reh gaye ticket channel mein. Mast bakchodi thi.

Par is chaos mein bhi, ek kami thi. KingOP ki. Hum chahte the ki wo wapas aaye, ki wo dekhe ki humne kya banaya hai. Hum chahte the ki wo humein roast kare, ki wo humein bole "Bhai ye kya chutiyapa kar rahe ho?" Aur ek din, wo wapas aaya. VC join kiya. Wo KingOP tha, par wo KingOP nahi tha. Wo Zynx tha.

Zynx
Ye kya kar rahe ho tum log? Server destroy kar rahe ho? Ismein kya maza hai? Tum log bohot bachpana kar rahe ho. Technologiaa... ye kya naam hai? Bohot cringe hai.
Uski awaaz mein wo purani warmth nahi thi. Wo cold tha, judgemental tha. Wo aisa baat kar raha tha jaise hum koi stranger hain, aur uski duniya mein humari koi jagah nahi hai.
Alone
Maza hai bhai! Chaos mein maza hai! Tu wapas aa gaya, chalo, ab hum teeno milke phir se waisa hi karenge! Wahi purana time wapas!
Zynx
Nahi. Main ye sab ke liye wapas nahi aaya. Mujhe kuch aur karna hai. Aur tum dono ko bhi serious hona chahiye. Main Moonlit pe kaam kar raha hoon, Zoe ke saath.

Usne humari bakchodi mein join hone se mana kar diya. Wo wahi baitha raha, silent, jaise hum usse bohot niche hain. Usne humein bataya ki wo ek naya 'chill' server bana raha hai—Moonlit. Humne pucha, "Bhai, humein wahan owner banayega?" toh usne bohot coldly jawab diya, "Zoe ne mana kiya hai." Hum dono wahan reh gaye, shocked. KingOP ab humara best friend nahi tha, wo sirf Zoe ka bodyguard ban gaya tha. Usne humein ignore karna shuru kar diya, aur ye humare liye sabse painful tha—ek aisa best friend jo wahan hai, par wahan nahi hai.

Zynx ne apna naam change karke "Zoe's Bodyguard" rakh liya tha. Moonlit server mein hum sirf spectators the. Humne dekha ki kaise wo ek insaan se ek stranger mein badal gaya. Usne humein ignore karna shuru kar diya, aur ye humare liye sabse painful tha. Wo wahan baitha tha, hamari VC mein, par uska mind aur heart kahin aur tha. Wo dosti ka aakhri stage tha—jahan tumhe realize hota hai ki tumne use permanently kho diya hai, aur tum kuch nahi kar sakte.

Zynx ek aisa persona ban gaya tha jo hamari saari memories ko erase karne ke liye taiyaar tha. Wo humein roast nahi karta tha, kyunki roast karne ke liye dosti ki zaroorat hoti hai. Woh humein bas 'tolerate' karta tha. Aur wo tolerance—wo humari dosti ka sabse bura version tha. Humne Technologiaa banayi thi, chaos create kiya tha, par wo sab useless tha agar wo humein waisa nahi dekh sakta tha jaisa wo pehle dekhta tha. Moonlit server ki wo coldness, wo Zoe ka influence, aur KingOP ka wo strange transformation—sab milke ek aisi deewar ban gaye the jise hum tod nahi paaye.
