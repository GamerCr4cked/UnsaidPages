Chapter 7: Unearthing

Zynx ke chale jaane ke baad, wo silence itni bhari thi ki hume laga hum ghut jayenge. Us raat, Technologiaa ke us khokhla aur bejaan server mein, main aur Alone (Flexxy) sirf do ghosts ki tarah baithe the. Humne wo hackers wali bakchodi band kar di thi. Hamein realize ho gaya tha ki hum KingOP ko zinda nahi kar rahe the, hum bas ek aise insaan ke saath interact kar rahe the jo humein bhool chuka tha. Alone ne apna headset table pe patka aur ek lambi saans li.

Alone
Bhai, mujhe nahi lagta ki wo wapas aayega. Zynx ne KingOP ko maar diya hai. Wo Zoe ke saath itna obsessed hai ki usne humein... humein waste samajh liya hai. Humne Technologiaa banaya, chaos create kiya, par wo humein waisa nahi dekh pa raha jaisa hum pehle the. Humne usse kho diya hai, Unknown.
Uski awaaz mein pehli baar wo gussa aur 'high' khatam ho gaya tha. Sirf ek gehra dukh bacha tha.

Maine kuch nahi bola. Mere paas bolne ke liye kuch bacha hi nahi tha. Maine apni screen pe wo purani photos dekhi—humari trio ki, ThorSMP ke us sunset ki, Alone Hosting ke launch ki. Wo photos kisi aur ki kahani lag rahi thi. Main aur Alone wahan baithe ghanto tak ro rahe the, andar se. Humne ek dusre ko wo sab bataya jo humne kabhi nahi kaha tha—humara darr, humara disappointment, aur wo void jo KingOP ne piche chod diya tha. Alone ne bataya ki kaise wo har roz KingOP ke purane messages padhta tha, aur kaise use lagta tha ki wo shayad kuch galat kar gaya.

Me
Nahi, Alone. Hum galat nahi the. KingOP abhi bhi wahan hai. Wo bas us Zynx wale persona ke peeche dab gaya hai. Wo suspended ID hi keys hai us purane KingOP ki. Hum haar nahi maan sakte. Agar humne haar maan li, toh wo permanently Zynx ban jayega.

Humne us raat decide kiya ki hum KingOP ko wapas layenge. Humne purani yaadein taazi ki, aur humein realize hua ki wo purana KingOP tab tha jab uska wo ID active tha. Shayad wo ID wapas aane se, wo persona bhi wapas aa jaye. Humne appeal karne ka decide kiya—ek aakhri, desperate attempt. Humne apne purane logs nikale, support tickets ki screenshots ikatthi ki, aur ek aisi appeal likhne ki tayyari ki jo unhe ye bata sake ki ye sirf ek account nahi hai, ye ek dosti ki jaan hai.

Us raat hume realize hua ki humari dosti sirf gaming ya hosting ke baare mein nahi thi. Humne decide kiya ki hum har cheez ko unearth karenge. Purane conversations, purane moments—sab kuch. Hum KingOP ke liye ladenge, kyunki wo humare liye ladta tha. Humne us raat ek mission tayyar kiya. Humne tayyar kiya ki hum kaise us suspended ID ko wapas layenge. Wo raat ek dosti ka maut ka safar nahi, balki dosti ko punar-janm (rebirth) dene ki koshish thi.

Humne us raat promise kiya tha. Ek aisa promise jo humare dil ke bohot close tha. Humne tayyar kiya ki hum KingOP ko wapas layenge, chahe uske liye humein kuch bhi karna pade.
