Chapter 5: The Downward Spiral

Bunny ke saath closeness badh rahi thi—ya shayad main bas yahi sochna chahta tha. Har ek DM notification mere liye ek adrenaline rush tha, aur har ek 'seen' message mere liye ek anxiety ka pahad. Main usse propose karne ke liye har pal taiyaar rehta tha, par us din, jab maine apni saari himmat jutakar wo words type kiye, mera heartbeat mere kaan mein zor-zor se baj raha tha. Maine 'Send' dabaya aur screen ko ghoorne laga.

Me
Bunny, I know humne bohot baatein ki hain, aur main ye kehne mein thoda nervous hoon... but I really like you. Kya tum mere saath—like, more than just friends—hona chahti ho?

Kuch minutes ka sannata tha, jo 10 saal ke barabar laga. Par jawab aaya: "Nahi."

Bunny
I'm sorry Unknown, par main tumhe bas ek friend ki tarah dekhti hoon. I don't feel the same way.

Wo "Nahi" mere dil mein ek dagger ki tarah laga. Rejection ne mujhe andar se tod ke rakh diya tha. Main depression mein chala gaya—wo wala depression jahan tumhara dimaag har waqt tumhe tumhari galti yaad dilata hai. Maine server se, VC se, doston se—sabse doori bana li. Par tabhi mujhe pata chala ki mere doston ne mujhe kitne messages bheje the.

Alone
Bhai, Unknown, tu kahan mar gaya hai? Server crash ho gaya hai, aur tu gayab hai? Wapas aa!
KingOP
Unknown, phone utha bhai. Bunny bhi bohot worried hai. Kya hua hai tujhe?

Jab main wapas aaya, tab Bunny ne accept kar liya! Par mujhe laga ye ek prank hai. KingOP aur Alone mujhe tease kar rahe the.

Alone
Hahaha! Dekha? Prank tha bhai! Tu sach mein ro raha tha kya? Hahahaha!
KingOP
Bhai, hum teeno ne decide kiya tha ki tujhe thoda frustrate karenge. Relax kar!

Mujhe laga ye teeno milke mera mazak uda rahe hain. Isliye maine uske proposal ko seriously nahi liya aur ignore kar diya. Par woh prank nahi tha—aur wo galti mujhse ho gayi. Wo galti jo meri poori dosti ki foundation ko hila ke rakh dene wali thi.

Maine Bunny ko ignore kiya, socha ye koi game hai. Maine Hosting pe wapas concentrate kiya, jaise kuch hua hi nahi. Par Bunny mere ignore karne se hurt ho gayi thi. KingOP aur Alone ko bhi laga ki main change ho gaya hoon. Hum teeno ke beech wo pehli darar thi—ek misunderstanding ki wajah se jo humein kabhi nahi thi.

KingOP
Unknown, tu Bunny ke sath kya kar raha hai? Wo bohot hurt hai. Tu behave kyu kar raha hai jaise tujhe farak hi nahi padta?
Me
Mujhe farak padta hai ya nahi, wo mera matter hai. Tum dono beech mein mat aao.

Hum teeno ke beech wo pehli darar thi. Bunny mere saath friendly hone ke bajaye, KingOP ke saath zyada friendly hone lagi. Woh dono ghanto tak baat karte, server pe jokes share karte. Main side-line ho gaya tha.

Main akela mehsoos karne laga tha. KingOP ke saath meri baat-cheet kam ho gayi thi. Alone ab bhi roasting karta tha, par usmein wo pehle wali warmth nahi thi. Aur tab, KingOP ne Bunny ko propose kar diya. KingOP! Mera best friend! Woh propose kiya aur Bunny ne koi reply nahi diya, bas Discord se leave kar gayi. KingOP tab shocked tha, depress ho gaya. Hum teeno ke beech ki dosti mein ek ajeeb sa tension aa gaya tha. Bunny ke us leave karne ne humein teeno ko tod ke rakh diya tha. Tab life ne humein busy kar diya. Hum teeno alag-alag direction mein bhagne lage. KingOP tab fully depress ho gaya tha aur usne Discord chod diya. Uske bina, Alone Hosting ka koi matlab nahi tha, toh humne server band kar diya. Alone bhi offline ho gaya.

Main akela reh gaya, active. Tab Alone wapas aaya. Hum dono ne Discord pe ghanto baatein ki, purani yaadein taazi ki, aur tab humein laga ki humein wapas kuch bada karna chahiye. Humne ek nayi team banayi—Technologiaa.
