Chapter 8: The Unsaid Ending

Appeal submit ho chuki thi. Monitor ki wo blue light ab meri aankhon ko chubhne lagi thi. Kamre mein wo khamoshi thi jo sirf tab hoti hai jab tumne apni poori zindagi ka ek bada hissa ek button click par laga diya ho. Wo sannata jo un logo ke beech hota hai jo jante hain ki unhone apni jaan laga di hai, aur ab decision kisi aur ke haath mein hai. Hum dono VC mein baithe rahe—main aur Alone—screen ki us loading icon ko ghoorte hue, jo shayad kabhi na rukne wala tha.

Me
Pata hai Alone, kabhi-kabhi lagta hai ki hum KingOP ko nahi, balki us purane version ko dhundh rahe hain jo shayad ab exist hi nahi karta. Kya hum kisi marne wale insaan ko zinda karne ki koshish kar rahe hain?
Alone
Shayad. Par bhai, kya humare paas koi aur option hai? Ya toh hum Zynx ke stranger hone ka dukh jhele, ya phir hum umeed rakhein ki wo purana KingOP wapas aa jaye. Maine wo 'password69' wala joke abhi bhi note mein save karke rakha hai. Main usse kabhi nahi bhulunga.

Maine Alone ki baat suni aur mera dil aur bhari ho gaya. Ye dosti ab ek survival ban gayi thi. Hum KingOP ke liye nahi lad rahe the; hum apne us saal ke liye lad rahe the, us dosti ke liye lad rahe the jahan Zoe nahi thi, jahan Moonlit server nahi tha, jahan sirf hum teeno the.

Zynx... wo naam ab mere dimaag mein ek ghoonsa maarta tha. Kya wo humein pehchaan payega? Kya wo humari "Technologiaa" wali bakchodi pe wahi purani hansi hasega? Ya phir wo Zoe ke saath Moonlit server mein busy rahega, humari appeal ko dekh kar bhi ignore kar dega? Ye uncertainty—ye "kya hoga" wala sawaal—humari har saans mein ghul chuka tha. Humne 5 mahine wait kiya tha, aur wo 5 mahine ek eternity ke barabar the.

Raat ke 4 baj gaye. Hum dono thak chuke the, par humari aankhein screen se nahi hat rahi thi. Humne imagine karna shuru kiya ki agar KingOP wapas aa gaya, toh hum pehla message kya bhejenge. "Bhai, kaha tha tu?" ya "Bhai, server crash ho gaya hai, jaldi fix kar!" Humne wahi purani bakchodi rehearse ki, jaisa hum 5 mahine pehle karte the. Par wo rehearse karte waqt humein pata tha—ye sab ek jhooth ho sakta hai. Hum ek aisi kahani likh rahe the jo shayad kabhi puri hi nahi hogi.

Ye dosti ka ek aisa mod hai jahan humein bas wait karna hai. Ek aakhri umeed. Ek aakhri login. Shayad yahi humari kahani ka asli ending hai—wo ending jo abhi unsaid hai. Agar wo appeal reject ho gayi, toh Zynx humesha ke liye Zynx rahega. Agar accept ho gayi... toh shayad wahi purana KingOP wapas aa jaye. Hum wait kar rahe hain, monitor ke blue light ke saamne, ki kab wo notification aaye. Aur tab tak, hum sirf ek unsaid ending ke saath jee rahe hain.

Maine apni chair piche ki aur chair ke haath pakad liye. Alone ne mic unmute kiya. "Unknown?" usne dheere se pucha. "Haa?" maine jawab diya. "Jo bhi ho, Technologiaa humesha rahega, hai na?" Usne pucha. Maine muskura ke kaha, "Humesha." Chahe KingOP wapas aaye ya na aaye, hum teeno ki wo dosti, wo struggle, wo hosting ka time... wo sab humare dilon mein hamesha zinda rahenge. Humne us unsaid ending ko accept kar liya tha, aur shayad, yahi is kahani ka sabse honest ending hai.
