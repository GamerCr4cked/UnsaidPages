Chapter 2: ThorSMP & OPSMP

Tab sab kuch bohot simple tha. Zindagi mein hosting, database, zoe, ya identity crisis jaise cheezein nahi thi. Bas Minecraft tha, ek village, aur totems ki zarurat thi. ThorSMP ka wo din mujhe aaj bhi yaad hai—it was chaotic, messy, aur exactly waisa tha jaisa hona chahiye. Main raid ke liye spam kar raha tha. I was desperate. Mere paas resources nahi the aur village raid ke liye humein ek team chahiye thi. Tabhi screen pe chat box mein ek naam pop hua: KingOP.

KingOP
Bro, main help karta hu. coordinates bhej.

Wo akela aaya. Maine socha tha ki raid ke liye 3-4 logon ki team lagegi, par KingOP ne bina kuch kahe mere saath raid puri ki. He was efficient, calm, aur sabse zaroori—he didn't steal the totems. ThorSMP mein jab aksar log loot ke liye ek dusre ko dhokha dete the, KingOP ka wo approach mere liye shock tha. Raid khatam hone ke baad hum ek mountain top pe khade the, sunset dekh rahe the, aur tabhi humne baatein shuru ki. Wo sunset humari dosti ki shuruat thi—ek aisa moment jahan humein laga ki ye koi temporary teammate nahi hai, balki ek aisa partner hai jiske saath main safe feel kar sakta hoon.

Us raid ke baad, humne apni Discord IDs share ki. Us raat ki baat-cheet mein wo trust build hua jo maine online kisi ke saath feel nahi kiya tha. Woh sirf ek teammate nahi tha; woh samajh gaya tha ki mujhe kaise playstyle pasand hai. Humne ghanto baatein ki, par minecraft ke baare mein kam aur zindagi ke baare mein zyada. Pata nahi kab raat ke 3 baj gaye, par humein pata tha ki humein ek dusre ka partner mil gaya hai. Tab wo 'KingOP' tha, ek aisa ladka jo sirf gamer nahi, balki ek insaan tha jo support system ban sakta tha. Humari dosti tab ek saaf chashme ki tarah thi—koi confusion nahi, koi hidden agenda nahi. Bas hum the, aur humari baatein.

Kuch hafte baad, KingOP ne mujhe OPSMP join karne ka invite diya. Main join hua—wahan ka infrastructure bohot zyada sophisticated tha. KingOP ne wahan kaafi setup kar liya tha. Jaise hi me enter hua, bina kisi "hi-hello" ke, mere screen pe ek notification aaya: "You have been promoted to Owner."

Me
King? Ye kya hai? Owner? Maine toh bas join kiya hai! Ye kyu kar raha hai?
KingOP
Bhai, mujhe pata hai tu kya kar sakta hai. Owner le, humein aur bhi servers manage karne hain. Trust hai mujhe tujhpe. Hum milke kuch bada karenge.

Maine chat check kiya, wahan ek aur player tha—Alone. Uski role bhi 'Owner' thi. Wo server ka environment bohot different tha. Alone wahan ke system ko manage kar raha tha, aur uska approach bohot chaotic tha. Woh commands ke saath khelta tha, admins ke saath mazaak karta tha, aur pura server atmosphere ko light rakhta tha.

Alone
Bhai, ye naya Owner kaun hai? King, tu kisko ghusa raha hai apne server mein? Ye toh naya lag raha hai.
KingOP
Chill mar, ye Unknown hai. Ab se hum teeno hi sambhalenge sab. Trust me, he knows his stuff.

Alone ka wo roasting approach mujhe bohot pasand aaya. He was suspicious but funny. Maine pehli baar Alone se baat ki thi, aur wahi se humari trio bani. Tab humein nahi pata tha ki ye teeno ka bond aage jaake "Alone Hosting" jaisa kuch bada khada karne wala hai. Hum bas teen players the jo ek saath milke duniya conquer karna chahte the—ya shayad bas ek saath rehna chahte the. Tab humhari dosti sirf ek game server tak limited nahi thi; hum teeno ne milke ek aisi community banayi thi jahan humara control tha, humari rules thi, aur humari bakchodi thi.

Hum teeno ka VC routine ban gaya tha. Raat ko OPSMP ke bugs fix karna, ek dusre ke base pe prank karna, aur wo constant roasting... wo cheezein bas ek memory nahi thi, wo humari dosti ka blueprint tha. Humein nahi pata tha ki ye dosti kitni jaldi complex hone wali hai, ya kaise ye hosting service humein har mod pe test karegi. OPSMP ke wo din, jab hum sirf server-mates the aur zindagi mein tension sirf totems ki thi, wo humari zindagi ka sabse khubsurat phase tha. Tab dosti sirf trust pe thi—aur us trust mein koi Zynx nahi tha, koi Moonlit server nahi tha, sirf hum the—ek dusre ke liye.
