Chapter 4: Bunny

Hosting grind apne peak par tha. Users ka influx badh raha tha, tickets ki line lambi ho rahi thi, aur hum teeno ne shifts divide kar li thi. Mere dimaag mein tab hosting aur server performance ke alawa kuch aur nahi tha—ya kam se kam main ye jhooth khud ko bolta tha. Tabhi ek din, support ticket ke notification ne screen ko flash kiya. Bunny.

Bunny
Hi, mera server start nahi ho raha hai, aur main bohot confused hu ki settings kaise configure karu. Can someone help? 🥺

Tickets toh roz aate the, par Bunny ka message kuch alag tha. Na koi complaint thi, na koi demand—bas ek genuine confusion aur ek emoji. KingOP aur Alone ne mujhe message kiya: "Jaa Unknown, Bunny ko help kar. Meri toh coding se dosti nahi hai, aur King busy hai database debugging mein." Maine Bunny ko VC mein join karne ke liye kaha. Jab wo VC mein aayi, uska mic thoda static-y tha, shayad kisi purane headset ka issue tha, par uski awaaz mein ek strange calmness thi—ek aisi awaaz jo us chaotic VC mein ek oasis jaisi lagi.

Humne 30 minute tak uska server config check kiya. Woh baaki users jaisi nahi thi—wo patience se sun rahi thi, har step ko notes ki tarah follow kar rahi thi, aur jab humne endlich server fix kar diya, toh usne jo 'thank you' bola, wo bohot sincere tha. Wo normal se zyada friendly thi, aur humare roasting atmosphere mein wo ek surprise thi. Usne humein thank you kaha, aur bina kisi formality ke humari baaton mein participate karne lagi. Tab maine pehli baar notice kiya—uske presence se VC ki energy change ho gayi thi. KingOP aur Alone bhi suddenly thode 'polite' ho gaye the.

Us din ke baad, wo baar baar server mein aane lagi. Pehle ticket solving ke bahaane, phir bas chat karne ke liye. KingOP ne dekha ki wo kitni active aur helpful hai, toh usne Bunny ko staff role offer kar diya. Woh team ka hissa ban gayi. Phir maine uske saath chatting shuru ki. Woh bohot nice thi—bohot zyada. Par mere friends... wo toh mere friends the. Unhone jaldi hi notice kar liya ki main Bunny ke saath zyada engage ho raha hoon. Aur wahi se dosti ka wo equilibrium disturb hona shuru hua.

Alone
Unknown bhai, itni flirting? Hosting ke liye aaye the ya dating app hai ye? 😂
KingOP aur Alone VC mein mere aur Bunny ke private messages pe tease karne lage. Woh humein roast karte, Bunny ko embarrassment hoti, aur main? Main bass has ke baat taal deta. Par andar se, main uncomfortable ho raha tha. Wo roasting ab sirf mazaak nahi thi; wo ek competition jaisa feel hone laga tha.
KingOP
Bhai, Unknown ka blush mic se sunayi de raha hai. Seriously, itna mat gir, host server pe focus kar!

Main hass raha tha, wo hass rahe the, aur shayad Bunny bhi hass rahi thi. Wo waqt bohot khubsurat tha—jab hum sab ek team ki tarah the. Bunny humare beech ek aisi energy laayi thi jo humari dosti ko aur bhi vibrant bana rahi thi. Wo roasting sessions aur bhi zyada fun ho gaye the. Humein laga ki Bunny sirf ek user nahi, balki humari team ka ek permanent part hai. Humein tab koi idea nahi tha ki ye dosti ke beech ka woh pehla chota sa crack ho sakta hai.

Humne Bunny ke saath bohot saare late-night sessions spend kiye. KingOP kabhi-kabhi Bunny ko tech tips deta, Alone usse mazaak karta, aur main... main bas uske har message ka wait karta. Wo flirting humare VC routine ka hissa ban gayi thi, aur humne isse dosti ke ek naye level ke roop mein dekha. Bunny ka presence ne humein teeno ko ek dusre ke aur close kar diya tha, par saath hi, usne humare beech ek unspoken competition bhi shuru kar diya tha—woh competition jo dosti ko andar se khokhla kar deta hai. Wo flirtatious messages, wo late-night DMs—ye sab ek aisa trap tha jiski wajah se humari dosti ki priorities shift hone lagi thi. Tab hum teeno ko laga ki hum teeno milke Bunny ke saath team-up kar rahe hain, par asliyat mein hum teeno ek dusre se door ho rahe the.
